Three entailments: a stack, not three copies

Unifying by output vocabulary — because the three are different logics at different altitudes.

canon · 2026-07-25 · DL subsumption · model-checking · abductive GAP · the shared carrier

canon has three things called "entailment," and the shared word invites the wrong fix — merging them. That would be a forced abstraction: they are three different logics at three altitudes, not duplicates. The honest unification is to see them as a stack and give them one output vocabulary so they compose. This page is the glossary + the carrier.

The three, by altitude

MechanismLogicWorldAnswersModule
SubsumptionDescription Logic (OWL / HermiT)open what entails what at the type level — Kerberoasting ⊑ CredentialAccess. Produces the necessity edges / rules. semantic-core/reasoning.py
Model-checkingpropositional M ⊨ φclosed does the data satisfy the rule — a pruned boolean fold over the atom-truth artifact. detection/entailment.py
Abductive GAPnecessity + observability— given anchor ⊢ expected, classify the expected's absence against what's collected. detection/entailment_gap.py

They compose as a stack

Not competitors — a pipeline. Each layer feeds the next:

  DL subsumption   →   the necessity RULES        ("Kerberoasting is a CredentialAccess";
  (reasoning.py)       (type-level entailments)     "a MiniDump spawn ⊢ an lsass read")
        │
        ▼
  model-checking   →   evaluate a rule on data     (M ⊨ φ over the atom-truth artifact)
  (entailment.py)      (does it hold here?)
        │
        ▼
  abductive GAP    →   classify the MISSING         (entailed but absent-while-observable
  (entailment_gap)     against observability          → GAP; absent-and-unobservable → NONE)

The Kerberos page shows all three in one domain: subsumption gives golden-ticket ⊑ forged-TGT, model-checking evaluates the issued-vs-presented rule, and the GAP layer reads a presented-without-issued ticket as an entailed-but-missing issuance.

The shared carrier — one vocabulary

What unifies them is the output. Each answers "does the evidence support the consequent, and with what epistemic status?" and maps into one carrier (detection/entailment_verdict.py):

VerdictMeaningBelnap
CONFIRMEDentailed and holds / observedTRUE
REFUTEDcontradictedFALSE
GAPentailed, but its evidence is absent while observable — the entailment/observation divergence (abductive-only)TRUE + a telemetry-gap warrant
NONEno claim — unobservable, or (open-world) not entailed either wayNONE
The load-bearing distinction — open vs closed world. Closed-world model-checking can REFUTE from absence: a rule that does not hold is false. Open-world DL subsumption cannot — a subsumption that is not entailed is UNKNOWN (NONE), never refuted, absent an explicit disjointness axiom. So the same "not present" fact maps two different ways: 
from_model_check(False)  → REFUTED     # closed world: absence refutes
from_subsumption(False)  → NONE        # open world: absence is unknown
That asymmetry is the entire reason the carrier needs both REFUTED and NONE — and it's why a single merged "entails()" would be a lie. The adapters keep each mechanism's epistemics honest.

The gap-vocabulary, reconciled

"gap" appears three ways in canon; they are related points on a "reachable-but-unwitnessed" spectrum, now named consistently:

  • GAP (this carrier) — entailed but its evidence is missing while the channel is collected. A real forgery/evasion signal. Projects to Belnap TRUE + a warrant.
  • coverage-gap / observability-gap (the orchestrator) — a statement about detector coverage, not about a fact: no detector is registered, or its data isn't collected. A different axis; maps to NONE in this carrier (no claim).
  • Belnap NONE (the provenance carrier) — the substrate value the above resolve into: "we can't say."

So: GAP is the only one that carries positive evidence (the step happened); the coverage/observability gaps are honest NONEs. Keeping them distinct is what stops "we didn't see it" from masquerading as "it didn't happen."

What was built

Not a merge — a unifying layer that leaves the three mechanisms untouched: detection/entailment_verdict.py defines the carrier (CONFIRMED / REFUTED / GAP / NONE) and three adapters (from_subsumption, from_model_check, from_gap) that map each mechanism's native output into it — encoding the open/closed-world epistemics — plus entailment_to_belnap to project onto the substrate fold. The three modules keep their own tested interfaces; this is the thin waist that lets them compose.
