Ground ⇄ Stitch ⇄ Test: the abduction loop

How a Splunk Notable becomes a grounded narrative — and where the LLM is irreducible vs where it must not go.

canon · design conversation · 2026-06-10 · the investigation-DAG loop, re-derived from proposer/verifier · designed, not built

Start from "LLM proposes, verifier grades" and push on it, and it does not collapse into "the verifier does everything." It collapses into a loop, and the hinge is one distinction: verification (deductive, a query) vs abduction (generative, inference to the best explanation). The verifier owns the first completely. Only the second is the LLM's, and it must be kept small.

Two acts, and only one of them is the LLM's

verification — deductive

Does a stated claim hold? "Did rundll32 access lsass?" "Is this really T1003.001?" This is a query against data + ontology + primitives, returning a Belnap value. No LLM needed — including to grade the SIEM's own MITRE tag, which is just another stated claim. The verifier can do the whole of this.

abduction — generative

Which of 10,000 grounded-true facts cohere into a story, and what does that story imply? There is no query for "the best explanation" — the space of explanations is open. This is the LLM's irreducible job, and the one thing the verifier genuinely cannot do.

Why you can't just "find all the valid proposals" — two walls

The tempting inversion is: enumerate every grounded triple first, then let the LLM stitch a narrative out of them. It breaks on two walls:

  1. The interpretive space is unbounded. The atomic facts in a log are finite. The valid proposals — typings, attributions, technique-relevances — are every entity × every class it might be × every technique that might apply. That is combinatorial; you cannot materialize it. Enumeration needs a frontier, and a frontier needs a hypothesis to scope it.
  2. TRUE ≠ relevant. Facts aren't a story. A host has thousands of grounded-true facts; almost all are benign. The verifier confirms each, but confirmation isn't relevance. The narrative is the selection of which facts cohere — and that selection is a hypothesis.
So you don't remove the hypothesis step — you move it, from "propose facts" (where the LLM hallucinates) to "select-and-connect grounded facts" (where it's safe, because it's only ever handed truths). That move is the whole win.

The loop

Neither proposer-first nor enumerate-first. The recursion is the engine: the hypothesis scopes the grounding, the grounding feeds the stitch, the stitch's gaps drive the next grounding.

GROUND (in the current frontier) verifier surfaces the grounded atoms in scope [deductive · no LLM] e.g. proc:rundll32@HOST-7 rdf:type d3f:Process = TRUE │ ▼ STITCH LLM assembles a candidate narrative from those atoms, [abductive · LLM] and names what is missing "comsvcs LOLBin dumped lsass on HOST-7, creds reused outward" │ ▼ TEST the narrative IMPLIES new atomic claims not yet grounded "if cred-dump → lateral movement, expect auth HOST-7 → HOST-9" → those become the next grounding queries │ ▼ EXPAND ground the implied claims → loop ── until ── ✓ fully grounded (the story holds) ~ remaining gaps are honest NONE (out of telemetry — unobservable, not absent) $ cost budget spent (stop, report what's open) ! a BOTH appears (data contradicts the story → escalate)

The narrative's gaps drive the enumeration — which is why you never ground everything up front: you only pay to verify what some candidate story actually needs.

Division of labor — keep the LLM surface minimal

Three actors, and the LLM is the smallest:

  • verifier — verification. Query over data / ontology / primitives → Belnap. The whole of "does this hold."
  • framework graph — known progression. The kill-chain / Attack-Flow graph encodes the transitions (cred-access → expect lateral-movement). So the TEST step's "what should follow" is mostly a graph traversal, not the LLM.
  • LLM — abduction only: which grounded atoms cohere into what story, the novel connective leaps the graph doesn't encode, and rendering the result in language. Nothing else rides on the fluent-but-untrusted component.
The narrative is the loop's output, not an input to be graded. It is grounded into existence atom by atom, then tested as a whole — so it never gets further than its weakest sub-claim. A confident story with a NONE in its spine stays a story with a hole, named.

Two failure modes to design against

propose-facts-then-check

Letting the LLM assert the atomic facts and checking after. It hallucinates atoms and the verifier spends its time refuting. Fix: hand the LLM only grounded atoms — it composes, it does not assert.

ground-everything-then-stitch

Verifying the whole log before stitching. Burns verification on thousands of irrelevant truths. Fix: let the hypothesis scope the grounding — enumerate lazily, guided by the narrative's gaps.

This is the investigation-DAG, from the other side

The ground ⇄ stitch ⇄ test loop is the same structure canon's design notes reach by a different road: the dynamic investigation DAG (nodes = whole findings, edges = "finding F triggered check M"), the cost-aware firing-order search, forward-progression + backward-walk, the reachable-NONE frontier (blast radius). Two derivations, one shape — which is the encouraging part: it means the architecture has one engine, not several. Designed in the notes; not built in the code.

Open directions (forward — not yet designed). The loop is the cell; the rest is fan-out and priority over the same cell.
  • Entry points. Canon attaches anywhere there is a claim to verify or a surface to ground against — a SIEM Notable, raw logs, a detection-rule corpus (grade the rules), an asset/environment graph, a TTP feed. Many places to "enter"; the loop is the same.
  • Dynamic attention. What to check first, and what's worth the cost, is a priority = risk × information ÷ cost, conditioned on the environment. Hand-coded cost-ordered greedy is the strong baseline; a learned attention earns its place only where non-greedy lookahead exists (expensive-A unlocks cheap-decisive-B).
  • Many agents, one graph. Independent devices/nodes investigate in parallel — the loop fanned out over the reachable frontier, per budget/intelligence. Parallel where independent, sequential only on the surface→verify→escalate spine. The natural scale-out — and the easiest place to over-engineer before the single cell is real.
