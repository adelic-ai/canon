Entailment: two ways up the mountain

The LLM proposes; canon verifies — but proposes what? Hypotheses (A) or rules (B).

canon · thinking capture · 2026-07-24 · the A→B story, the two LLM roles, the six structure options, and the audit that says they're already built

"The LLM proposes; canon verifies" was said before the entailment work firmed up, and it hides an unresolved fork: what the LLM proposes. Mode A — the LLM proposes an instance-level hypothesis about what happened, trusted per-incident. Mode B — the LLM proposes reusable rules, validated once, and a finite entailment structure does the reasoning. They are two different ascents of the same mountain. This page pins the distinction, the story order, the hypothesis object, and the six structural choices for B.

The one line to leave with. In Mode A the LLM's creativity lands in the verdict. In Mode B it is quarantined at the rule-authoring boundary — proposed offline, validated before it is trusted — and the online reasoning that produces verdicts is deterministic entailment. The LLM never touches a live conclusion.

The two modes

Mode A — LLM proposes hypotheses (the pre-entailment framing)

The LLM is the hypothesis generator. Point it at a bag of detections and it outputs an instance-level conclusion: "this is Kerberoasting into lateral movement toward the DC." Canon then checks that story statistically (the battery) and provenance-wise (the justified verdict). The hypothesis space is unbounded.

  • Strength: catches novelty; open-ended; instantly legible (the "agentic SOC" pitch everyone already understands).
  • Weakness: a hallucinated conclusion lands in the verdict; and the LLM can't tell you which hypotheses it failed to generate. No completeness. Canon can reject a bad proposal but can't know one was missed.

Mode B — LLM proposes rules (entailment)

The LLM never says what happened in this incident. It proposes type-level structure for the engine — reusable machinery a human or a validation step checks once, after which a deterministic entailment engine applies it to every incident forever. Examples of a "rule":

  • a new entailment edge — "a comsvcs MiniDump spawn ⊢ a VM_READ to lsass" (you cannot dump credentials without reading lsass memory);
  • an equivalence — "these two Sigma rules are the same rule" (see rule_equivalences);
  • a transition — "technique T emits under tactic T′", or a tactic→tactic edge in the kill-chain graph.

The hypothesis space is then finite and structured — the entailment closure over the attack graph — and each hypothesis is a scoreable object, not a sentence the model invented.

The story order — brief A, then B

A leads because it is the handhold everyone already grips; B is where you actually stand. The transition is the argument:

  1. Open on A (briefly). "You point an LLM at your alerts and it tells you what happened." Everyone nods — it's the 2026 agentic-SOC pitch.
  2. Land the discomfort. You cannot put an LLM's guess in a verdict, and it can't tell you what it missed. A room full of skeptics feels this immediately.
  3. Turn to B (the relief). So demote the LLM from oracle to rule-proposer. A finite entailment structure generates and scores the hypotheses, reports CONFIRMED / ENTAILED-BUT-MISSING / UNOBSERVABLE, and the LLM is nowhere near a live conclusion.
This is the same "abduction proposes, deduction verifies" split already stated in semantic_stack and witness_and_check, and the "conscience, not brain / accountability, not intelligence" point in agent_as_analyst — here sharpened into a story order: A is the ramp, B is the road.

The hypothesis object in B

A Mode-B hypothesis is concrete and low-dimensional. The working parameterization:

  • closeness to a crown jewel — position on an entity-path toward a high-value asset (the completeness axis of kill-chains as entity-path analysis);
  • probability of intrusion — the HMM/Markov likelihood of the hidden progression;
  • the identities involved — which principals/accounts the path runs through.

So a hypothesis reads: "identity E is on a path toward crown-jewel C, at completeness K, with intrusion-probability P." Enumerable, groundable, and — the differentiated part — complete relative to the ontology: entailment tells you not only what is present but what is entailed-but-absent.

The three epistemic outcomes (from the abduction loop's TEST step, made executable in experiments/entailment_test.py). An anchor motif entails a dependent one; replay the anchor and the dependent's presence/absence classifies three ways:
  • CONFIRMED — the entailed motif is present; the claim grounds TRUE.
  • GAP — absent, but its channel is collected → it happened (abduced from the anchor), the record is missing → telemetry gap / evasion. The entailment rules out "it didn't happen."
  • NONE — absent and the channel is uncollected → unobservable → no claim.
The NONE branch is the point: the system refuses to manufacture a verdict when it cannot see. Absence becomes evidence without overclaiming.

The six structure options for B

How does B represent and score the finite hypothesis space? Six structural choices, all present in the code or the design. Five are mechanisms; the sixth is the container the others nest inside. (Frames from the W-coordinate system and design/verdict_coverage_space.md.)

#StructureHypothesis / scoreWhere it lives
1 State-space / probabilistic sequence (HMM-Viterbi + Markov) hidden tactic path from technique emissions; score = likelihood. → the intrusion-probability axis. detection/hmm.py, killchain.py (orphaned)
2 Entity-path / graph a path from an entity toward a crown jewel; score = completeness × abnormality. → the proximity + identity axes. detection/chain.py, kill-chains-as-entity-path
3 Concept lattice / FCA a closed concept; entailment = the Duquenne–Guigues implication basis (complete) → the GAP with a guarantee. semantic-core/fca.py
4 Description logic (OWL / HermiT) class membership; entailment = subsumption (Kerberoasting ⊑ CredentialAccess). ATT&CK/D3FEND already in OWL. semantic-core/reasoning.py
5 Propositional model-checking (M ⊨ φ) a formula; entailment = model-satisfies-formula, a boolean fold over the atom artifact. detection/entailment.py (orphaned)
6 Coverage-space coordinate model — the container observation space (entity×time×feature — anomalies are points) × state space (the kill-chain graph — campaigns are trajectories), bridged by the W-record. Verdict = truth-at-a-location; chain = trajectory; detectors = independent witnesses. design/verdict_coverage_space.md, W-coordinate system
How they nest. Options 1 and 2 live inside the state space of (6); options 3/4/5 do the entailment at each location; the whole thing carries a Belnap four-valued × log-odds-confidence value (the two orthogonal verdict axes — don't collapse them). Mapped to the parameters: proximity + identity → (2); intrusion probability → (1); entailment / GAP / completeness → (3/4/5); located and combined by (6). So the decision isn't "which structure" — it's which is primary and which are layers, and verdict_coverage_space.md already argues for (6) as the container.

The two open forks

  • Which entailment layer? FCA (complete, boolean), DL (rich, open-world), or model-checking (precise, but you author φ). They sit at different altitudes — propositional / description-logic / abductive — and are not redundant, but the shared word "entailment" invites confusion. Worth a one-paragraph glossary.
  • Is a hypothesis the 3-tuple, or a path scored by it? The 3-tuple is elegant but possibly lossy; an attack-path scored by (proximity, probability, identity) is richer and matches (2)+(6). Lean path.

The convergence: B's spine is already built — and loose

A looseness audit of canon (2026-07-24) found that its three loosest things are the same failure mode: a module built, tested, and documented as shipped that the live path routes around. Those three modules are exactly the entailment spine B needs:

  • detection/entailment.py (structure 5) — tested, docs say "Built," but round.py reaches past it into atoms.fire_factored, bypassing the prune/short-circuit that is its whole reason to exist. Dead on the live path.
  • detection/hmm.py (structure 1) — exported, but nothing live calls it; the orchestrator uses the first-order Markov forward_nexts, never the HMM. Orphan.
  • semantic-core/validation.py — a SHACL wrapper only tests call; the live path uses provenance/shacl.py. Dead twin (flagged a year ago, still true).
So "sharpen canon" and "build toward B" are the same move: wire the entailment/HMM code the live path currently ignores. That is not adding features — it is connecting what is already built and tested. The highest-leverage tightening (resolve entailment.py, wire or demote hmm.py, delete the validation.py twin) is the first construction of the Mode-B engine.
Banked as clean (stop tracking): the omega rule-projection soundness bugs are fixed and regression-pinned; and "provenance is accountability, not intelligence" is enforced in code (unsigned custody → NONE, reference inputs marked "NOT evidence") — not marketing.

One line

A is the trailhead everyone knows and has no summit guarantee; B is the harder, narrower route whose payoff is that when you reach a verdict you can prove what it rests on and what it doesn't. Retire "the LLM proposes; canon verifies" as the headline and replace it with: the LLM proposes rules; entailment does the reasoning; canon certifies what the verdict rests on.
