Atoms, combination, and the why

The frame under all of canon: typed, sourced atoms; combinations checked local and global; against a (who, job) root.

canon · the keystone · 2026-06-12 · what every cell and readout is an instance of

Strip canon to its smallest shape and it is this: atoms combine, every combination has a why, and detection is recognizing the combinations whose why doesn't trace to a legitimate root. Co-occurrence, derivation, taint, orphans, drift, routing — all of it is this one question at different scales. This is the entry point; the rest are instances.

1 · Atoms — tagged by source × type

An atom is the irreducible unit — an event, a fact, an instruction, a piece of content. Two tags carry almost all the weight, and the basics are the basics:

  • source — where it came from: a host process, a user prompt, an email, a downloaded PDF, a web result. This is the derivation root at the boundary — what decides trusted vs untrusted.
  • type — what kind it is, and the load-bearing distinction is instruction vs data. An instruction-typed atom from an untrusted source is the whole danger in one tag-pair.

Dublin Core gives source and type natively; the rest of the description (subject, coverage, creator) tags the context cheaply and in RDF, so it composes with PROV-O and CID for free.

2 · Combination — checked two ways, local and global

Atoms combine: a content atom derives from a source, an instruction triggers an action, events join into a motif. Every combination has a why — and the why is read at two scales that turn out to be the two halves of canon:

local why — authority (derivation)

Does this combination trace to a legitimate authorizing root? An instruction authorizing an action is legitimate iff it derives from the legitimate who. Mechanisms: derivation, orphan (missing required ancestor), taint (untrusted source → sink). The verifier.

global why — drift (attention)

Is the running pattern of combinations still on-job? Has the agent's subject / activity drifted from its baseline? Mechanism: subject & activity drift over a moving baseline (memoria). The surfacer.
The two are interdependent, not just compatible. The goal is improvement-from-your-own- past (the global, drift side). But improving from un-vetted past conclusions is self-poisoning — so the local authority check (provenance/derivation) is the safety rail that keeps the improvement loop from feeding on forged or drifted atoms. You cannot safely do the global without the local. A real injection fails both: one combination authorized by the wrong who (local) and a pattern bent off-job (global).

3 · The why bottoms out in a (who, job) root

What authorizes a combination? It traces to the legitimate who (not the process account — who is directing) and the job (the basic directive). In PROV-O this is native: a prov:Agent acting on a prov:Plan via qualifiedAssociation. An injection is an activity not associated with the legitimate plan — it follows the attacker's, and its authority traces to the email / the PDF / the web result instead. Every predicate collapses to one question: is the agent acting for the right who, on the right job?

This is the one genuinely hard, out-of-band part. The atoms (source × type) and the combination checks (derivation, orphan, taint, drift) are buildable and partly built. Pinning the legitimate (who, job) is not — it must be declared by the operator or learned over time (the moving baseline). The frame doesn't hide this; it points right at it. Everything else is "does this combination, local and global, trace to that root."

And it applies to itself: the system's own conclusion-atoms combine into better future analysis — improvement-from-past — and the same local check keeps that self-improvement honest. Generalize the frame, then apply it onto anything, including the analyzer.

The one line. Atoms tagged source × type; combinations checked two ways — local authority (derivation) and global pattern (drift) — against a (who, job) root that is the single hard thing to pin down. Improvement-from-past is the goal, provenance is the rail, and a real injection fails both scales of the why.
