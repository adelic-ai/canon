Canon — where it stands

After the inversion reframe: surfacer + verifier + fidelity-checking.

canon · state readout · 2026-06-05 · main 405be89 · branch detection-otrf-first-cell

This session sharpened the architecture and tested two of its load-bearing assumptions against real data. The design got dramatically more coherent; the build did not keep pace. This is an honest readout of what is proven, what is designed, and what is inert by design — because the gap between those three is the real story.

The reframe, in one line

Canon shifted from "self-validating detection substrate (with a battery)" to a sharper shape: the battery is one of two search directions (bottom-up surfacer of statistical shape); the frameworks are the other (top-down Sigma/CAR/ATT&CK verifiers of known content); the W-record routes between them; and the substrate's distinctive job is to emit justified verdicts and check the verifiers' own fidelity. Continuous with "substrate is the value, detectors are a vehicle" — but it finally says what the substrate does with the frameworks: active routing + fidelity attestation, not passive coverage mapping.

Why two directions. The battery sees shape (volume, distinct, timing) and is blind to low-volume, content-specific acts. The frameworks see known content and are blind to the novel. They cover each other's blind spots. Proven on real data below.

Three buckets — the honest state

BUILT + PROVEN load-bearing now, on real data

  • The uniform justified-verdict substrate across 3 detector families (539 tests) — pre-session.
  • First cell on real OTRF/Mordor data (committed + pushed). It proved three things previously only asserted: (1) the pipeline runs end-to-end on a diverse, ATT&CK-labeled corpus that does not collapse; (2) the substrate emits honest verdicts on real data; (3) the fidelity-checking thesis is real — on an LSASS credential-dump, both the statistical battery and the off-the-shelf Sigma rule missed the comsvcs/rundll32 LOLBin; only checking the exact mechanism caught it.
  • Two probes converted to evidence: routing SPARQL is fast (bind-the-seed, single-digit ms on the real D3FEND graph); OTRF/Mordor cleared as a usable diverse labeled corpus (52 techniques, NDJSON, per-event, bucketable).
The one piece of genuinely new ground. "Check the detector's fidelity" went from claim to demonstration. The off-the-shelf rule's filter_generic trusts all of system32 to cut false positives — so a LOLBin abusing a trusted system binary walks right through it. Canon caught the gap by diffing the rule against the allowlist-off mechanism, and recorded why the rule missed in the verdict.

DESIGNED coherent, not built (held)

  • The real inversion pipeline: battery → W → retrieve verifier from the framework → fire → Belnap. The cell hand-wired this; the actual W→framework retrieval is the seam the whole architecture rests on and is the one thing the cell faked.
  • Milestone orchestration / two-direction search / firing-order policy (the "tops" = ATT&CK tactics; kill-chain sequences the escalation; parallel where independent, sequential on the surface→verify spine).
  • The "primitive cannon" = Atlas (catalog/relations) + the verification substrate (verified bindings + transparency knob + guarantee tiers); the self-populating-then-gated Atlas agent.
  • The per-rule fidelity attestation as a STIX object distributed over TAXII (canon-novel); the layer / data-component axis built into objects; deterministic field→clause human-prose rendering.
  • The agent-substrate vision (LLM proposes, canon verifies); TDA/imaging/ML-sequencing (spitball tier).

INERT BY DESIGN honest-NONE, contributing nothing now

A real fraction of canon's folds are correct but currently silent — they ask questions the available data can't answer. This is honest (they report NONE rather than faking a result), not a defect — but the currently load-bearing core is narrower than the architecture's surface area.

Claiming any of them as a live differentiator today is overclaiming until that wiring exists.

Atlas and the seams, specifically

  • Atlas: unchanged — still thin, still paused. What moved is its justification: the session named its consumer (the firing-order / primitive-selection search) and reframed the cannon as Atlas + substrate. So Atlas went from "parked, no consumer" to "consumer identified, not yet pulled." Progress in why, zero progress in content.
  • Seams / protocols: the contracts/ base is built (CID, carrier, fold protocol, custody, guarantee certificate, verdict schema, SHACL). The session added to the protocol thinking — STIX/TAXII as the cyber-domain narrow-waist borrow (the distribution seam) and the layer/data-component axis — but those are design additions, not built. The narrow-waist philosophy held: STIX/TAXII slotted in as "the cyber borrow," exactly as PROV-O / in-toto / SHACL did.

What is now settled

  • Detection sophistication is dead as the value prop. Confirmed again on OTRF: the attacker was visible by name (winx64_payload.exe); the statistics drowned it under benign system shape. Cheap beats sophisticated, on a third real corpus. The value is firmly substrate + fidelity-checking + honest-NONE, and that is now shown on real data.
  • The one genuine research problem is W→framework observable-vocabulary alignment — mapping a surfaced anomaly's observables to the framework's data-components/techniques. Everything else is bounded engineering.
  • The currently load-bearing core is narrow: the justified-verdict substrate + the fidelity-gap demonstration. The rest is honest scaffolding awaiting data, infra, or build time.
Canon's position in one sentence. A coherent, mostly-designed architecture with a small but now-real proven core (justified verdicts + demonstrated fidelity-checking on real data), several deliberately-inert folds awaiting external wiring, one open research problem (alignment), and a design-to-build gap that the next phase should close by building rather than designing.

The cleanest next move

Not more architecture — wire the actual W→framework retrieval: battery surfaces a candidate → retrieve the real local Sigma/CAR rule → fire it → Belnap-aggregate → justified verdict. That is the seam the whole inversion rests on, the one thing the first cell faked, and the local content already exists (semantic-cyber/data/sigma-rules/: 10 lsass rules, 15 tagged T1003.001; CAR analytics on disk in ~/dev/csat).
