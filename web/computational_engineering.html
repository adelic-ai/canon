Canon as computational engineering

Leap71 encodes physics to compute rocket engines. Canon encodes the physics of inference to compute detections — logic as the law, data as the test stand.

canon · exploration · 2026-06-21 · the Leap71 trajectory, the formal toolbox, and what's achievable without ML

Leap71 doesn't CAD a rocket engine — it encodes the engineering reasoning into a model (Noyron) that computes the geometry from requirements; the aerospike fired first try because the physics is faithful. The inversion that matters: the model is the asset, not any one engine. Canon is on that trajectory — with one domain difference that sets every real target.

The crucial divergence — there is no predictive physics of intrusion

Leap71 has physics → first-time-right + a cheap definitive test (fire the engine). Detection has no closed theory of "is this an intrusion" — its "physics" is logic (sound) + adversarial empirical reality. Two targets follow:

"First-time-right" → "honestly-warranted." Canon can't be correct-because-physics; its analog is the verdict carries its warrant and its uncertainty truthfully. Don't chase a certainty the domain can't give.
The test stand is the credibility-maker, and the bottleneck. Leap71 was believed because the engine fired. Canon is believed when computed detections demonstrably catch real attacks while staying clean on real benign. That is why the data work (bots-v3) is not a side-quest — it IS the engine test stand.

Logic as the physics of INFERENCE — not of the phenomenon

Belnap (the 4-valued carrier), SKOS (graded relations), FCA (concept lattices), entailment, information theory, probability — these are canon's "physics," but of inference and evidence, not of attacks. They govern how to combine, propagate, and qualify evidence soundly. They make the empirical grounding rigorous; they don't replace it.

The logic is the lens; the data is the light. Building the formal structure rigorously is building the physics — of reasoning — and it's the non-reinventing path provided we use the established tools. "Logic is our physics" must not slide into "so we don't need the data."

What canon is circling — the detection vision

The "wands" — a finite atom basis instead of spray-and-pray

3700 independent rules factor into the small set of shared atoms (predicate × field) they're built from. Evaluate the basis once → a content-addressed atom-truth artifact → every known rule is a boolean composition of already-computed atom-truths, data-free. Finite + complete for the known corpus (new techniques mint new atoms — not "every possible detection").

Two inference modes

DEDUCTIVE   fire atoms → entailment → definitive detections     ("what's there"; true/false; a Datalog program)
ABDUCTIVE   partial/weak signs over long time → P(intrusion)    ("what's likely"; the low-and-slow; honest-NONE made continuous)
            over the kill-chain graph — discriminated by COHERENCE (entity-linked, ordered, crown-jewel-directed), NOT count
They compose over the attack-graph: definitive detections pin nodes hard, abductive accumulation fills the rest with graded belief, crown-jewel-directed paths are where you read off "likelihood of intrusion here." Detections factor along shared graph-edges the way rules factor into shared atoms — two factorings, same shape.

The resolution axes — and the instance caveat

Resolution rises claim → structure → behavior. Five axes (what a rule detects; how rules relate; the carrier; cross-schema mapping; why a rule missed), plus two the five miss because they're all static and input-side: trajectory/temporal (point → sequence → kill-chain) and warrant-depth (absent → well_formed → bounded → machine_checked — "guessed" vs "proven" anomaly, the substrate's spine). Live refinements: cross-corpus ablation and catch-profile (record why each fired, not just which).

The treated corpus is an instance — warrant-is-relational turned inward. But only the behavioral/catch-set layer is the instance; the structural treatment is portable. Invest machinery on the portable half (free wins); behavioral resolution is data-bound. Cross-corpus ablation measures which is which — and doubles as the honest-scoring mechanism for any learning loop.

The ML loop — the realistic version

ML doesn't invent structure; it searches a space you frame, scored by a metric you ground. The buildable loop: a generative proposer (Fable 5) emits candidate refinements → apply each → score by the two-sided fidelity delta on a held-out / cross-corpus background → keep improvements. Propose-verify, with grounded_fidelity as the verifier. Cross-corpus scoring keeps it honest — else it teaches to the test (the same circularity as two-instances-one-driver).

The goal — better than the median engineer, without ML

Achievable where formal rigor beats human inconsistency: consistency, coverage-honesty (NONE not FALSE), calibration, traceability — a large fraction of the actual job, and where median practice (inconsistent, over-claiming, spray-and-pray, uncalibrated) loses structurally. Not achievable without the generative half: novelty, org-context, adversarial creativity.

So: beats the median on execution, not creativity — exactly the Leap71 pattern. Noyron out-executes the encoded physics; it doesn't out-create the engineer. "Without ML" is coherent because Noyron isn't ML either — physics-as-computation; canon's analog is logic-and-statistics-as-computation.
Everything reduces to two bottlenecks. The wands, the abductive posterior, the ML loop, the resolution axes — all of it reduces to a generative proposer (Fable 5, the imagination) and a grounded metric on differently-shaped data (the test stand). The formal structure is the lens; these two are the light and the calibration. Build the test stand; the rest is structure on top.
