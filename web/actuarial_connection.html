The actuarial connection

A closer analogue than LEAP71 — and one concrete borrow canon has needed all along.

canon · borrow lead · 2026-06-05 · methods → Atlas; FIBO = finance's CASE/UCO

Actuarial science is the mature discipline for canon's actual problem: calibrated decisions on diffuse distributions, from accumulated empirical evidence, honest about irreducible uncertainty, with a human-in-the-loop control cycle. An actuary never predicts whether you specifically die next year — they calibrate the distribution from population data and price the tail. Canon never proves this process is malicious — it calibrates from evidence and acts on the tail. Same epistemics, a century of rigor.

The honest disanalogy

Actuarial risk is stationary and non-adversarial — your house doesn't strategically burn down to defeat your insurer's model; mortality tables are stable. Cyber's adversary is adaptive and non-stationary — the attacker changes behaviour to evade the model, so the distribution shifts adversarially. Actuarial machinery assumes the distribution stays put.

Borrow the math, mind the gap. Take the calibration / credibility / tail methods; the adversariality is game-theory / adversarial-ML territory, not actuarial. Same discipline as every canon borrow — the method transfers, the domain assumption does not.

RDF in the actuarial / insurance world

  • The modelling is not RDF — actuarial practice is R / Python (chainladder) / specialist software (Prophet, AXIS). No RDF in the core workflow.
  • The data + regulatory layer does have semantic standards:
    • FIBO (Financial Industry Business Ontology, EDM Council + OMG) — a genuine RDF/OWL finance ontology. The strongest "RDF here" answer.
    • ACORD — the dominant insurance data-exchange standard, but XML (with RDF-mapping efforts).
    • XBRL — regulatory financial reporting (Solvency II), XML with RDF mappings.
The clean parallel. FIBO is to finance what CASE/UCO is to cyber — the domain vocabulary for describing the entities, in RDF/OWL. If canon ever touches finance (it is on the cross-domain list), FIBO is the borrow. Confidence: high that FIBO is RDF/OWL; verify ACORD/XBRL RDF-mapping specifics before citing.
