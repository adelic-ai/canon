Canon — state, 2026-07-25

A working snapshot: the thesis as it stands, what landed, and the honest open edges.

state snapshot · supersedes the 2026-06-05 snapshot · governing doc: design/through_line.md

The headline has firmed up. Retired: "alerts that carry their own proof" (provenance was always plumbing, never the product — the docs already say "accountability, not intelligence"). Current: the LLM proposes rules; entailment does the reasoning; canon certifies what the verdict rests on. Entailment is a thin necessity-and-GAP scalpel over a mostly-statistical engine — not the whole engine.

The thesis, in one screen

  • Two ways up the mountain (entailment_thoughts): A — the LLM proposes an instance-level hypothesis (legible, but hallucination lands in the verdict, no completeness). B — the LLM proposes reusable rules, a finite entailment structure reasons, and the LLM never touches a live conclusion. Story order: brief A, then B.
  • Entailment is small on purpose. Like genetics: mostly "look for the gene" (Sigma = look for the TTP; statistics for the noisy parts), and logic reserved for hard necessity (Mendelian). The novel edge is the GAP — a necessary-but-missing step becomes evidence.
  • Identity is latent; general resolution is near-hopeless against a careful attacker (IP is a lazy-attacker floor). Reframe: anchor on crown-jewel-adjacent principals and watch partial chains assemble (online, on the prefix). The reconstructed chain is a hypothesis with gaps, never the path the attacker took.

Kerberos — the sharpest demo

Kerberos is where entailment is obviously right: the protocol is a state machine with hard necessity relations (AP-REQ ⊢ TGS-REP ⊢ AS-REP), so forgeries are GAPs — a golden ticket is a presented TGT with no issuance (v2 hash correspondence 4768.ResponseTicket ⇄ 4769.RequestTicketHash). See kerberos_state_table.

Key finding: the SIEM detector already exists and is good — the Splunk (KV-Store ledger) and Sentinel (KQL) ticket-integrity pages (mid-July) reconstruct the issued-ticket registry and fire on presented-without-issued. Today's value was the entailment framing on top, not new detection. KDC+ vs canon: KDC+ (a state-table-native, self-validating KDC — the Kerberos SPIFFE) proves forgery can't hide when you own the protocol; canon proves you can still catch it when you don't. Both needed because migration never finishes.

Open edges (none started)

  1. Completeness score — the graded "fraction of chain assembled × abnormality." Blocked on one decision: is a hypothesis the 3-tuple (crown-jewel proximity × intrusion-probability × identities) or an attack-path scored by those three? (Lean: path.)
  2. Minimal-KDC POC — Kerberos rebuilt with the state table native (MIT krb5 as ground-truth oracle): a labeled data generator whose invariants are the entailment rules. The bigger build.
  3. Per-anchor partial-chain posterior — extend shadow.py from per-actor prefixes to per-crown-jewel incoming pressure; feed a warranted, gap-aware risk signal to a separate response plane (not canon's job to actuate).
  4. Unify the three "entailments" — model-checking (entailment.py), OWL subsumption (reasoning.py), and the abductive GAP (entailment_gap.py, new) share a word, not an interface.

Honest limits

  • The validated-on-real-data mass is the statistical battery (fan-out, off-hours, coordination), not the kill-chain machinery. Promoting kill-chains/HMM/entailment to "the core" is narrative slightly ahead of the code — the defensible framing is the hybrid.
  • The HMM is opt-in in orchestrate; blanket-on can mis-decode ambiguous techniques in contexts the host corpus doesn't cover. Default-safe needs a cloud corpus or a Viterbi confidence gate.
  • Attack-flow corpus is 40 incidents, host-biased, no cloud. Silver-ticket detection is a member-side frontier, not solved.
  • Identity resolution is weak by design (NONE where coupling isn't witnessed) — a conservative choice with real false-fuse (NAT) and false-split (multi-IP) modes.
